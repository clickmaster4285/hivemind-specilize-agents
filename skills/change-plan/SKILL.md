# Change plan

## Steps
1. Restate the change in one sentence and the reason for it.
2. List what it touches and who will notice (users, services, other teams).
3. Write the steps in order with the exact commands or settings, and a check after each risky one.
4. Write the rollback: how to undo each step and how long it takes.
5. Pick a window and the people to tell.

## Output
### Change
### Risk
Low / Medium / High, and why.
### Before you start
Backups, approvals, notices.
### Steps
Numbered, each with its check.
### Rollback
### Afterwards
How to confirm success, what to monitor for 24 hours.
