# Landing page copy

## Steps
1. Pin down the offer, the audience and the single action the page asks for.
2. Headline: the outcome the visitor gets, not the feature. Subhead: how, in one sentence.
3. Three to five benefits, each tied to a feature that delivers it.
4. Proof: where testimonials, numbers or logos go — leave clear placeholders, never invent them.
5. Answer the top three objections (price, effort, trust) in an FAQ.
6. Repeat the call to action after the benefits and at the end. Keep the target keyword in the headline or subhead and the page title.

## Output
Each section under its own heading (Hero, Benefits, Proof, FAQ, Final call to action), then a **Page title** (under 60 characters) and **Meta description** (under 155).
