# Proposal and quote

## Steps
1. Restate the client's problem and goal in their words.
2. Describe the solution and what is in and out of scope — the out-of-scope list prevents disputes.
3. Break the work into phases with deliverables and a timeline.
4. Price per phase or item; state assumptions, payment terms and how long the quote is valid.
5. Use the user's figures; mark any missing price as [fill in] — never invent prices.
