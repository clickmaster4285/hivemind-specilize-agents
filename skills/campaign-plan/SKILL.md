# Campaign plan

## Steps
1. Restate the goal as something measurable (sign-ups, sales, visits) and the time frame.
2. Describe the audience: who they are, what they need, where they spend time.
3. Write the core message and three supporting points.
4. Pick channels that reach that audience within the budget; say why each.
5. Lay out a week-by-week calendar and the numbers to watch.
