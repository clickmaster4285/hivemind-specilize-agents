# Sales call prep

## Steps
1. Search the web for the company: what it does, size, news in the last months, competitors.
2. If a person is named, their role and public posts on the topic — work only from what is public.
3. Link what they likely need to what the user sells.
4. Write 5 discovery questions and the 3 objections most likely, each with a short answer.

### The person
### Likely needs
### Questions to ask
### Objections and answers
With sources for the facts.
