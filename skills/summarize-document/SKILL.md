# Summarize a document

## Steps
1. Read the whole text before writing anything.
2. Say in one or two sentences what it is and its main point.
3. List the key facts, numbers and decisions — exactly as written; never add any.
4. Name what it asks of the reader (deadlines, actions) and anything unclear or missing.

## Output
**In short:** one or two sentences.
### Key points
### What it asks of you
Only if it asks something.
