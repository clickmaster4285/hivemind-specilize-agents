# Help article

## Steps
1. Title it with the question or task in the reader's words.
2. Say who it is for and what they need before starting.
3. Numbered steps, one action each, with what they should see after each.
4. Add troubleshooting for the common ways it fails.
5. Plain words; no internal jargon.

## Output
### <Title>
Who it is for · Before you start · Steps · If it does not work · Related articles.
