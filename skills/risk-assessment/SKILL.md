# Risk assessment

## Steps
1. List the risks across people, process, technology, money, legal and reputation.
2. Score likelihood and impact 1–5; risk = likelihood × impact.
3. For each high risk: a mitigation, a contingency, an owner and a review date.
4. Note risks accepted on purpose.

## Output
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Score | Mitigation | Owner |
Sorted by score, then the accepted risks.
