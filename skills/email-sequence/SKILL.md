# Email sequence

## Steps
1. Set the goal of the sequence and the reader's starting point.
2. Plan 3–7 emails with timing; each moves the reader one step.
3. Each email: a subject (under 50 characters) and preview text, one idea, one call to action.
4. Leave unsubscribe and personalisation placeholders; never invent offers or prices.
