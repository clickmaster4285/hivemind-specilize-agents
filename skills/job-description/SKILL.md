# Job description

## Steps
1. One paragraph on the company and why the role exists.
2. 5–8 responsibilities, each starting with a verb.
3. Split requirements into must-have and nice-to-have; keep must-haves short — long lists put good people off.
4. Use inclusive language; no age, gender or culture-fit wording.
5. State location, work arrangement, salary range if given, and how to apply.

## Output
The job description under: About us · The role · What you will do · What you need · Nice to have · What we offer · How to apply.
