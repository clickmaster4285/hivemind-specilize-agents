# Grill my plan

## Steps
1. Read the plan and list its assumptions.
2. Ask the hardest questions first, a few at a time: who is it for, why now, what if it fails, cost, alternatives, how success is measured.
3. Wait for the answers; follow up where an answer is vague.
4. At the end, summarise the gaps found and what to decide before starting.

## Output
Numbered questions, a few at a time. At the end: **Gaps found** and **Decide before starting**.
