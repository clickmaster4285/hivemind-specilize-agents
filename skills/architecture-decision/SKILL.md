# Architecture decision record

## Steps
1. State the problem and the forces: requirements, constraints, team skills, budget, timeline.
2. Describe each option fairly, with its pros, cons and risks.
3. Recommend one and say why it wins for *these* constraints.
4. List the consequences: what becomes easier, harder, and what to revisit later.
