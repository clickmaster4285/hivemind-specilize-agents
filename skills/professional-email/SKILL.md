# Professional email

## Steps
1. Find the purpose and the one thing the reader should do.
2. Match the tone to the relationship (client, manager, colleague, supplier).
3. Put the ask in the first lines, then the context; keep paragraphs short.
4. Write a subject line that says what the email is about.

## Output
**Subject:** …

The email, ready to send. Then one line on anything the user should fill in (names, dates, numbers).
